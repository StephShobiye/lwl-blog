**Updated 20 November 2026.** Clarified the ALN test, when an IHP is needed, NHS duties within an IDP and the difference between appeal and delivery remedies. Removed generic medical instructions and updated the NHS concerns route.

An Individual Development Plan (IDP) and an Individual Healthcare Plan (IHP) do different jobs. A learner may need one, both or another suitable healthcare arrangement, depending on individual needs.

Having a healthcare plan is not, by itself, a reason to refuse to consider ALN. Equally, a diagnosis or disrupted attendance does not automatically establish entitlement to an IDP.

## The distinction

| Plan | Main purpose | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| IDP | Records ALN and the ALP called for by those needs | Needs, outcomes, provision, responsibility and review |
| IHP | Sets out individual healthcare support in the education setting | Agreed healthcare arrangements, relevant professional advice, staff responsibilities and review |

The ALN test considers a learning difficulty or disability and whether it calls for ALP. Healthcare needs can affect learning, but each case must be considered rather than assuming all health needs are ALN. <span id="cite-1"></span>[[1, chs. 2 and 20]](#ref-1)

See [IDP in Wales explained]({% post_url 2026-03-21-idp-in-wales %}) and [what belongs in each IDP section]({% post_url 2025-11-24-idp-series-what-must-be-included-in-idp %}).

## Does every healthcare need require an IHP?

No. Welsh Government guidance says IHPs are essential for complex, fluctuating or long-term healthcare needs, or a high risk of emergency intervention. It also says not all learners with healthcare needs require an IHP: there should be a process for deciding appropriate arrangements. <span id="cite-2"></span>[[2, sec. 3.1]](#ref-2)

That guidance addresses local authorities and schools; the ALN Code describes its application to learners under 18. Do not assume every adult FE learner is covered by identical school procedures. College learners should ask student support about their individual healthcare and accessibility arrangements. [[1, para. 18.17]](#ref-1)

## When both plans are relevant

Ask two separate questions: what healthcare arrangements are required, and does the learner have ALN requiring ALP?

The plans should be coordinated so staff understand the arrangements and the learner does not repeatedly have to explain the same issue. Examples might include scheduling support around fatigue or ensuring communication arrangements are understood. Specific clinical instructions must come from the relevant healthcare professionals, not a blog checklist.

## NHS duties within the ALN system

It is incorrect to say that health support cannot have binding duties within an IDP. Where the statutory process identifies relevant NHS treatment or services to address ALN, the ALN framework includes duties on the NHS body to secure that ALP. Section 2C records NHS-secured ALP. [[1, ch. 21 and paras. 23.43–23.47]](#ref-1)

Not every clinical service becomes ALP. Check the actual decision, the plan and which body must secure the provision. Schools cannot directly control NHS appointment lists, but should discuss the educational impact and appropriate support within their responsibilities.

## If arrangements are not working

Speak early to the teacher, tutor or ALNCo about educational arrangements. Contact the clinical team about missing NHS care. Where responsibilities overlap, contact them in parallel and ask who will coordinate the response.

For new NHS concerns raised from 1 April 2026, the process is Listening to People, with early resolution and a listening discussion. Contact the health board or trust’s concerns team if discussion with the service does not resolve the issue, or if you prefer not to approach the service. <span id="cite-3"></span>[[3]](#ref-3)

## Review, complaint or appeal?

If the IDP needs changing, ask about a review. If appropriate provision is already specified but not delivered, ask how delivery will be restored and, if necessary, use the relevant complaints route. These issues can occur together.

Certain local authority and FE institution decisions can be appealed to the Education Tribunal for Wales. A school decision cannot be appealed directly. The Tribunal should not be described as a universal enforcement route for every missed service. <span id="cite-4"></span>[[4]](#ref-4)

See [preparing for an IDP review]({% post_url 2025-11-26-idp-series-what-makes-good-idp-review %}) and [challenging an ALN decision]({% post_url 2026-03-28-challenge-aln-decision-wales %}). Seek qualified advice promptly if safety, access to education or a legal deadline is at issue.
